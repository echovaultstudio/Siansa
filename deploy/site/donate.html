Support Siansa

Siansa is free, open source and made in spare time. It costs nothing to use and never will. If it's part of your day and you'd like to give something back, a donation helps pay for what's behind it: the server the beta runs on, the Google Play account, the domain, and the hours.

Buy us a coffee on Ko-fi

One-off or monthly, whatever you like. Ko-fi takes no cut of one-off donations.

What a donation goes to

  • The server behind the beta: the F-Droid repository, problem reports and the sign-up page.
  • The Google Play developer account and the siansa.app domain.
  • Time: fixing what testers find, and the next features.

Other ways to help

  • Test it and tell us what's wrong: every report with a log makes the next version better (Settings › Help › Report a problem).
  • Code: the app is open source. Fixes and features are welcome as pull requests.
  • Tell someone who runs their own music server.

Donations don't buy features or priority, and there's nothing behind a paywall: everyone gets the same app.
