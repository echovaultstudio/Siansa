Siansa is in beta. It's still in active development. It has been tested a lot with Navidrome to iron out most bugs, but it isn't perfect yet, so expect some. If you find one, please report it from the app (Settings › Help › Report a problem). Thank you!

What Siansa can do

A music player for your own Navidrome or Emby server, with mixes made for the moment.

Mixes made for the moment

Daily mixes for each part of the day, and for the days you choose. They can take the weather, whether you're moving, the light and your headphones or car into account. Describe a mood or a sound ("rainy jazz piano") and get a mix on the spot. Optional AI only names each mix; your library never leaves your servers.

Radios and discovery

Song radio that stays close to its song, artist radios, similar songs, albums and artists by how they sound, and Song path: a queue that moves smoothly from one song to another. Roll the dice, rediscover albums you used to love, and find artists you don't have yet.

Your listening

Wrapped-style pages for this month, last month and this year, a time machine for any month you played, and your sonic fingerprint: what the music you play most sounds like. Songs you skip are left out of your mixes.

Scrobbling

Plays go back to your server and, if you like, to Last.fm and ListenBrainz-compatible services such as Koito. You choose when a song counts as listened, and hearts sync to your server and Last.fm.

A careful player

Gapless playback, smart crossfades that follow how songs start and end, even loudness, a ten-band equaliser, synced lyrics (word by word where available), a sleep timer that winds down gently, and playback on Chromecast and DLNA TVs and speakers.

Your library, on the go

Your library synced to the phone, downloads for offline listening, playlists on your server or only on the phone, favourite albums and artists, animated covers, several users and libraries, and backups of your settings and history.

The smart parts need AudioMuse-AI with the Siansa Analysis plugin.
