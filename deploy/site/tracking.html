Feature tracking

What Siansa works with today, and what's coming next. Ticked means it's in the app now.

Music servers

  • ✓ Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic servers (Gonic, Airsonic, Ampache and more)
  • ✓ Emby
  • Jellyfin
  • Music files on your phone

Audiobooks and podcasts

  • ✓ Audiobookshelf (books and podcasts, with your progress kept in sync)
  • Internet radio and podcast links (from an .m3u or a web address)

Playback

  • ✓ Gapless playback (live albums and DJ mixes play without a gap)
  • ✓ Smart fades (crossfades that follow how each song starts and ends)
  • ✓ Loudness levelling (every song at the same volume)
  • ✓ Ten-band equaliser
  • ✓ Synced lyrics (word by word where available)
  • ✓ Sleep timer (winds down gently)
  • ✓ Chromecast and DLNA (TVs and speakers)
  • Gapless playback while casting
  • Android Auto

Mixes and discovery

  • ✓ Daily mixes for each part of the day (can follow the weather, your activity and your headphones or car)
  • ✓ Mood mixes (describe a sound, like "rainy jazz piano")
  • ✓ Song radio and artist radios
  • ✓ Song path (a queue that moves smoothly from one song to another)
  • ✓ Similar songs, albums and artists (by how they sound, with AudioMuse-AI)
  • Your own covers for mixes, playlists and vibes
  • More accurate genres (from MusicBrainz and Last.fm)

Your listening

  • ✓ Your month and your year (Wrapped-style pages)
  • ✓ Time machine (any month or year you played)
  • ✓ Sonic fingerprint (what the music you play most sounds like)
  • ✓ Scrobbling (to your server, Last.fm and ListenBrainz-compatible services)

Your library

  • ✓ Library synced to the phone
  • ✓ Downloads for offline listening
  • ✓ Playlists (on your server or only on the phone)
  • ✓ Several users and libraries
  • ✓ Backups (of your settings and history)

Missing a server or a feature you'd like? Ask for it on GitHub; requests help decide what comes next.
