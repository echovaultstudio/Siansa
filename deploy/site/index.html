Siansa

A music player for your own Navidrome or Emby server, with mixes made for the moment.

Beta testers

  1. Google Play: open the invite link you were sent, tap "Become a tester", then install Siansa from Play.
  2. F-Droid: in the F-Droid app, go to Settings › Repositories › +, and add https://{{DOMAIN}}/fdroid/repo (or scan the QR code you were sent). Then search for Siansa.
