Siansa is in beta. It's still in active development. It has been tested a lot with Navidrome to iron out most bugs, but it isn't perfect yet, so expect some. If you find one, please report it from the app (Settings › Help › Report a problem). Thank you!

Siansa

A music player for your own Navidrome or Emby server, with mixes made for the moment. (More servers and features added soon.)

We believe in open source

Your music lives on a server you run yourself, and the app that plays it should be just as open. Siansa's code is free software under the GNU GPL: anyone can read it, check what it does with their server login, build it, fix it and pass it on. It's built on open-source libraries too, and we say which on the Open source page. If Siansa is useful to you, you can support it.

Join the beta

Why a closed beta?

Siansa is still early, so we're keeping the beta small on purpose. That way we can find bugs early, before lots of people run into them. We get to know the people testing, and we'd love testers who stick around and help shape the app, not just try it once. And we can hear your honest feedback: what you love, what annoys you, and what's missing. Thanks for being part of it!

Tell us a little about your setup and we'll send an invite. We only use your email for the beta (privacy policy).

For Google Play this must be the email of your Google account.
