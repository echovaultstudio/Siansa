### Changed
- **A Steady vibes DJ mix stays steady.** It's now held to the song it started from, not only to the last one: with Surprises at None, only songs within about 6 % of the first song's tempo and near its energy play, and half or double time no longer counts as the same tempo. A mellow house mix no longer walks into drum and bass after two songs. Each place of **Surprises** loosens this: a wider tempo and energy at A few, half and double time at Some, anywhere at Lots. Halftime drum and bass counts at its full tempo in a mix (with Siansa Beats 0.32 or newer). Steady build, Euphoric and Wind down are as they were.
