### Fixed
- **DJ blends that drifted or clashed (Siansa Dev).** Two songs are now beat-matched only when Siansa Beats has a grid for both of them, and only when each end really keeps to its grid: a song with a grid against one without gets a short blend instead of half a minute out of step.
- **No more pauses in DJ radio and DJ mixes (Siansa Dev).** A hard ending into a hard start now runs straight on, as a DJ would, instead of half a second of silence.
- **Ambient intros aren't skipped or "dropped" (Siansa Dev).** A next song whose beat only comes in later slides in under the old song from its start, and the old song is gone before that beat; an echo out is only used into a song whose beat starts right away.
- **Songs analysed after they were first played now blend on their grid (Siansa Dev).** Siansa Dev asks again for a song Siansa Beats hadn't analysed yet, after half an hour, instead of keeping its first readings until the app restarts.
