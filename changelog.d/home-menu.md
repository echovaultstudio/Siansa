### Changed
- **One menu at the top of Home.** The button beside the dice now opens a small menu with **Play on…**, **Sources** and **Arrange Home**. Press it and slide down to a row to open it in one go. While something plays on a TV or speaker, the menu says where and has a **Stop** button, and the button shows a small dot.
- **The dice rolls.** Tap it and it tumbles, keeps turning while it finds a song, and lands on a new face when the song starts.
