### Changed

- Flow has one bar. Where the mini player is, a pill in the mood's colour waits on the Flow page; press it and it
  folds into the mini player as the mix starts. No song list opens: you stay on Flow.
- A Flow mix can be steered while it plays: move the dot, change a sound, a genre or Artists, and the songs that
  come next change. It keeps its name, and ends when you stop or play something else.
- Now playing says "Now playing from" and the mix's name, the queue names it too, and + saves the whole mix as a
  playlist under that name. The queue's Shape your mix is hidden for a Flow mix unless a DJ mix runs.

### Fixed

- **A genre picked on Flow didn't lead the mix.** "More" of a genre now makes the mix from that genre's songs, and
  "less" leaves them out.
- **Genres were listed under Sounds too.** Sounds now has only sounds.
- **Lines showed in the colour at the top of Flow.** The glow is smooth now.
