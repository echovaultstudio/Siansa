- **Artist page buttons.** The heart to make an artist a favourite moved down beside the release count, on the left; Radio sits beside Shuffle and Play on the right.

### Added
- **A DJ mix of any list of songs.** On a radio, a mix, a card, Liked songs and every other list of songs, slide Shuffle onto Play for a DJ mix of just those songs, as on an album or a playlist. A list of more than 300 songs mixes 300 of them.
