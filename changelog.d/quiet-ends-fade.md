### Changed

- DJ blends: a song that winds down at its end fades into the next one over that quiet stretch (up to 12 seconds) where their beats can't be laid on each other and their keys don't clash. Where the end isn't soft, the old song's last bar echoes out on its own beat and the next drops in on its downbeat, as a DJ does it, instead of a blend of a second and a half or a dead cut. And after you move near a song's end, a short blend of what's left is made instead of none.
