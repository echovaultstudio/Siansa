### Changed
- **Sorting a list.** Liked songs and playlists sort with the same arrows and menu as Your Library, under the title, instead of a row of chips.
- **Genre pages.** The radio button is off the cover: it sits beside the heart, on the line with how many songs the genre has. Both grow into the title bar as that line scrolls under it.
- **The same on every page.** A page's own buttons arrive in the title bar the same way everywhere (an artist's and a book's too, and a book's page now has Play there). Every page whose cover Siansa draws (mood playlists, vibes, cards, song paths) has its name on the cover only and its Play in the cover's colour, as radios do. **New picks** is one button in one place on radios, mood playlists and cards.

### Fixed
- **Genres with no songs.** A genre with fewer than 30 songs by Siansa Beats' vote (Yé-Yé, Surf) opened an empty page when no song was tagged with it. Its voted songs show now.
