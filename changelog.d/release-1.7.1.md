<!-- release: 1.7.1 -->
### Added
- **Fades** (Settings › Playback): a short fade in when you press play and after you seek or skip, and a fade out when
  you pause or stop and before you seek or skip. A slider for each, from Off to 600 ms; 250 ms in and 150 ms out
  unless you change them. Only for what you start and stop yourself: gapless albums, crossfades and DJ blends play as
  before. With Untouched playback too: the song itself stays as it is, only the volume moves for that moment.

### Fixed
- Skipping a song no longer plays a moment of the old song in front of the next one, and a pause no longer leaves a
  blip that's heard when the music carries on.

Everything new in 1.7.0 is in this update too: Your listening on Home, new looks, pictures and colours for Home's rows,
Untouched playback, smarter DJ blends, pull Home down to load it again, and what's new with Siansa Beats (see 1.7.0
below).
