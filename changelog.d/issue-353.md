### Added
- **Your Stations: internet radio and podcasts.** A new Stations chip in Your Library. It shows your server's radio stations (Navidrome keeps a list), and you can add your own: a stream link, a .m3u or .pls playlist, or a podcast's RSS feed. Stations play live, with what's on when the station says; podcasts open their episodes. What you add stays on this phone.
