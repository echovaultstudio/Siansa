### Added

- **Mixes made from your listening, on Home.** With Siansa Beats 0.53 or newer, Home shows three shelves: **For now** (this part of the day, as you listen in it, and whether it's dark outside), **Made for you** and **More of you**. Each is a crate of records to flick through. Your own carousels come back when you switch it off.
- **Choose what Siansa Beats is used for.** Its card on Connections has a switch for each thing: scrobbling, loudness and fades, DJ blends, genres, similar music, moods and Home's mixes. Each is the same switch as on its own page.
