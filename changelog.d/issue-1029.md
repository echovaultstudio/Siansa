### Changed
- **An artist's page fades in over their picture.** As you scroll up, the page with the buttons, songs and albums slides over the picture with a soft edge, the picture staying almost still, instead of a hard edge cutting across it.
- **Release types are tabs at the top of an artist's page.** All, Albums, EPs, Singles, Live, DJ-mixes… sit right under the buttons and stay pinned under the title bar as you scroll. Pick one to see only those releases; All shows the whole page again. An artist with many types scrolls the tabs sideways.
