### Changed
- **Your mixes no longer change unless you ask.** Today's mix is made once a day; when the hour's vibe moves on,
  it stays until you tap refresh, and refresh makes one for the vibe of the hour. The home carousel works the
  same way: when a later part of the day begins, the mixes you have stay, with a **Make Your day mixes** button
  under them, and the new ones are only made when you tap it or one of their cards.
