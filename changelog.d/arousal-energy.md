### Changed

- DJ mixes and the sound pad go by how energetic a song is to a listener, not by how loud it is (with Siansa Beats 0.35). A loud but slow, heavy song no longer counts as high energy, and a hard one mastered quieter no longer counts as calm: Steady mixes hold their level better, and Steady build, Euphoric and Wind down climb and fall as you'd hear it.
