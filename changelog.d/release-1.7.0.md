<!-- release: 1.7.0 -->
### Added
- **Your listening, a new card on Home.** Your top five songs, artists and genres of the last 7 days, 30 days, year or
  all time, and Look back: this time last year, this day last year, last month and last year. Tap a list to open its
  songs. Pick its colour under its cog in Arrange Home.
- **New looks for Home's rows**, under each row's cog in Arrange Home: a hero card with 3 or 4 cards at its foot, a
  cover grid of any size (or one featured, big), a carousel in three sizes, new shortcut shapes and 1 to 4 rows of
  shortcuts.
- **Pictures and colours for every row's cards**: moving Aura, Frost, Poster and Glow pictures and three kinds of
  stickers, coloured from the covers, by the mood of the songs, or from a set.
- **Untouched playback** (Settings › Playback): each song at its own sample rate and bit depth, straight to your
  phone's sound output. Crossfades and the equaliser are off while it's on; it pauses itself for a DJ mix and comes
  back on afterwards.
- **Smarter DJ blends**: a three-band EQ, ridden a little differently each time. *With Siansa Beats 0.60 or newer and
  Find vocals on, two voices are never heard at once.*
- **Pull Home down to load it again**, with the Siansa logo turning above it.
- **What's new after an update**: the first time you open Siansa after an update, Home shows the biggest changes.
- Album page: hold the cover for **Clear all images**, to get the album's pictures again from the server.

- **New with Siansa Beats:**
  - **Siansa Beats learns from the songs you skip**: they count for less in your mixes. *Needs Siansa Beats 0.64.*
  - **Mixes named after the weather** ("Rainy Folk"), from one word, never your location. *Needs Siansa Beats 0.61.*
  - **Make the mixes again**, a button in Mix settings after you change one. *Needs Siansa Beats 0.60.4.*
  - **All mixes in one row on Home**, and how many cards, in Mix settings. *Needs Siansa Beats 0.62.*
  - **Siansa Beats is free, and keeps your listening too.** Its Listening page has your stats and charts: this month,
    your profile, and a time machine through your years and months. It takes your scrobbles from Siansa and can pass
    them on. From 1 December, Last.fm asks for a paid Pro subscription for an API key; Siansa uses a key of your own for
    Last.fm, so scrobbling there stops then unless you subscribe. Scrobbling to Siansa Beats stays free: Settings ›
    Scrobbling › Scrobble to Siansa Beats.

### Changed
- **Arrange Home**: rows show just their names, and each row's cog opens a tidier sheet (Show, Look, Cards) with the
  same choices for every row, and Reset this row.
- **Mix settings** are a page of their own, in three parts of one line each.
- **Home's mix rows** are the same three whoever makes the mixes, this phone or Siansa Beats: For now, Made for you
  and More of you.
- **Settings**: every page has a one-word name, and Streaming quality moved from Storage to Playback.
- Starting an album or playlist from one of its songs queues the whole list.
- About this song's Playing box says what the phone really does with the sound.
- Artist pictures are always round.
- *Siansa Beats 0.61 or newer:* finding vocals is optional and off until you switch on Find vocals under its Setup, as
  it makes the analysis much slower.

### Fixed
- **Shuffle really shuffles the queue**: the song playing stays first, the rest follows in a random order, and turning
  it off puts the list back.
- **Android Auto**: tapping an artist opens it (Play all, Shuffle and its albums) instead of playing everything.
- Names on cards no longer break in the middle of a word.
- A hero card follows its row's Card colour and Colours.
- A notice faded out beside Home's menu button no longer opens when you tap there: the tap brings it into view.
- DJ blends out of a track cut from a mixed CD no longer lay a long blend over the CD's own.
- A shortcut to an album whose picture was replaced on the server no longer shows the old one.
