### Changed
- **The DJ mix bar opens on any slide up.** With no DJ mix playing, slide up from the play button and the bar opens with **Start** in the middle: let go there for a DJ mix from the song playing, or on a shape to start one in that shape. While a mix plays, the middle is **Stop**, as before.
- **A slimmer DJ mix bar.** Only the symbols, without names (the cover still shows the name and what it does), with more room above the play button. It opens out of the seek bar's line and closes back into it in one quick, springy motion.
