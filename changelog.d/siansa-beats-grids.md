### Added
- **Beat grids from Siansa Beats (Siansa Dev, an experiment).** Under Crossfade › Beat-matched blends, "Beat grids from" can take each song's tempo, beats, bars and phrases from Siansa Beats instead of the Siansa plugin, with the plugin for the rest, or from Siansa Beats only. Loudness and fades still come from the plugin.
- **A playlist's own DJ mix (Siansa Dev).** On a playlist, slide Shuffle onto Play, as on an album, for a DJ mix of just its songs.
