### Changed

- Flow is new: drag one dot on the mood pad for calmer or livelier, slower or faster, and the page takes the
  mood's colour and name. Pick sounds and genres yourself with + (more of it, less of it, or off again), choose
  favourites or artists new to you, and tap Play this vibe. It now starts playing straight away.
- Genres can shape a vibe too: as Siansa Beats hears them, or from your songs' genre tags without it.
