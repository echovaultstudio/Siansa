### Changed
- **Album and playlist titles glide into the top bar.** As on an artist's page, the title under the cover now moves up with the page and glides into the top bar, shrinking as it goes. A long title is set a little smaller to fit one line.
- **Artist page: the heart in the top bar.** Once the artist's buttons have scrolled away, their heart shows in the top bar, so you can still make them a favourite.
- **Genre pages say what the genre is.** With Siansa Beats, a genre's page shows a short description with a little history, from Wikipedia, instead of repeating its name.
