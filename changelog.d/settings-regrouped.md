### Added
- **Settings › Services.** What Siansa talks to besides your music server, each with a switch: a service turned off is left alone and shown nowhere, while its login stays. **For the best Siansa** at the top says what answers, what's broken and what isn't needed (with Siansa Beats on, it offers to turn AudioMuse-AI off).
- **Find a setting.** A search box at the top of Settings: "battery" finds Pure black, "loud" finds Levelling, and a result opens its page with that section open and lit.

### Changed
- **Settings, regrouped.** The start page puts you first: who's signed in on what, your services with a green or red dot each, then twelve pages in three groups of four: Playing (Sound, Radios & mixes, Streaming & storage, Audiobooks & podcasts), Home & look (Home page, Theme, Now playing, Bottom bar) and Listening & app (Scrobbling & history, Backup & restore, Help & feedback, About). Style and its seven pages are gone: Theme has dynamic colours, the status bar and animated covers; Now playing has the buttons, the play button, the DJ mix bar and the mini player; Home page has the shortcuts, the notices, the menu's order and the cards. Old links still land on the right page.
- **Streaming and download quality** are drop-downs, and the **image cache** size a slider, so no words are cut off.
