### Added

- TV: a **Sync** pill on Home. It syncs your library without opening a page; its arrows turn while it runs and go red if it failed.
- TV: **Similar albums** on every album page and **More like this playlist** on playlists, with Siansa Beats as well as AudioMuse-AI.

### Changed

- TV: Home no longer has a Today's mix pill or a Recently played row: Today's mixes and Jump back in have them.
- TV: Today's mixes is a row of wide cards for each of the day's mixes (Morning, Daytime, Evening, Night or your own vibes): one made today plays, the others are made when you pick them. A mix that can't be made says why. **Settings › Today's mixes and vibes** is on the TV now.
- TV: Similar songs is a list under a small band in the colour of the song's cover, not a page with a large cover.
- TV: with the lyrics open in Now playing, they sit in a soft dark box, and the remote's guide and Siansa's logo step aside.
- TV: the Library's **Your sound** is called **Sonic fingerprint**, as on the phone, so it isn't mistaken for Home's Your sound mix.
- TV: the choices in a menu are pills, like every other button on the TV.
- TV: Settings › Streaming & storage shows the song cache Siansa keeps on the TV instead of sizes that did nothing there, and the remote reaches Clear song cache. The TV no longer shows a mobile-data choice for loading songs ahead.
- TV: covers no longer move, and the Animated covers setting is gone from the TV.
