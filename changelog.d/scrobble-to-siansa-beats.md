### Added
- **Scrobble to Siansa Beats (Siansa Dev).** With Siansa Beats 0.29 or newer set up, Settings › Scrobbling and history has a new switch: your listens go to Siansa Beats, which keeps your listening history and shows any month or year on its Listening page. It can take in an export of Koito or Last.fm, and send your listens on to Last.fm, with your hearts as loved tracks.

### Fixed
- **A wrong "Siansa Beats is too old" message.** When Siansa Beats couldn't answer for a song it hasn't analysed yet, the app said to update it. It now says what Siansa Beats said.
