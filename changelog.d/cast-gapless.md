### Added
- **Gapless casting through the phone.** In Siansa Dev, the cog beside a Chromecast in **Play on** has **How it
  plays**: **The TV plays the queue** (as before) or **Gapless through this phone**. In gapless mode the phone plays
  the music itself, with its crossfades, DJ blends and levelling, and sends the sound to the TV over the Wi-Fi as one
  lossless stream. The TV shows the song and its cover, and its remote works as before. The phone must stay on the
  Wi-Fi with Siansa running, and it uses more battery.
