<!-- release: 1.5.0 -->
### Added
- **Siansa Beats.** Siansa's own companion service for your server: it listens to every song once and tells the app
  its beats, loudness, key, genres and what it sounds like. Connect it on first setup or under Settings › Connections.
  It reads Navidrome, Emby and Jellyfin. AudioMuse-AI still works. More at siansa.app/beats.
- **DJ mixes on measured beats.** With Siansa Beats, every beat and bar of a song is known, so DJ mixes blend phrase
  on phrase. A DJ mix is offered only when it can work, and songs longer than 15 minutes are left out.
- **A DJ mix of any list of songs.** On a playlist, a radio, a mix, a card or Liked songs, slide Shuffle onto Play, as
  on an album.
- **The DJ mix bar.** While a DJ mix plays, slide up from the play button to shape it (Steady vibes, Steady build,
  Euphoric, Wind down) or stop it.
- **Genres by how songs sound.** With Siansa Beats, Search › Genres, each genre's page (with a short description) and
  genre radios go by what your songs sound like.
- **Scrobble to Siansa Beats.** Your listening history can live on your own server; Time machine reads it from there.
- **Settings › Services and Find a setting.** One page shows every service with a green or red dot and a switch, and
  Settings has a search box.
- **Dynamic colours on pages.** Artist, album and playlist pages take a calm colour from the cover, with one switch.
- **More ways to arrange Home.** Choose how cards are painted (Vivid, Soft, Plain or From the artwork), show any row
  or card as a shortcut grid, and keep favourite radios.
- **Play in the title bar** once a page's own buttons have scrolled away.
- **Android Auto: Like, Shuffle and Repeat** in the car's player, on the lock screen and in the notification.
- **A Bad blend button** (Settings › Help & feedback) to mark a blend that sounded wrong, for a report.

### Changed
- **Smarter blends.** Smart crossfade picks how to blend each pair of songs: a song that winds down fades into the
  next over its quiet end, a hard ending runs straight into a hard start, and long DJ blends of 32 bars are made.
- **Steady mixes stay steady.** They keep to the kind of music they started with, and go by how energetic a song
  feels, not how loud it is.
- **Shape your sound has a sound pad**: one dot for energy and speed, with dials that suit the mix.
- **Artist, album and playlist pages.** The title glides into the top bar, an artist's release types are tabs, and
  the heart and Radio sit with the other buttons.
- **Your listening, Time machine and Sonic fingerprint share one look**, and Time machine picks a year from a list.
- **Settings, regrouped**: you and your services first, with shorter explanations and switches that work the same
  everywhere.
- **Home's menu has a new look.**
- **Song path no longer has "Follow the lyrics".**
- **TV:** genres, DJ mixes and artist photos as on the phone; Home is there at once when you come back to it; and
  Siansa keeps far less on the TV's storage.

### Fixed
- **Music stopping on its own with the screen off** after a long pause.
- **Repeat or shuffle coming on by itself** with a car or headset on Bluetooth.
- **Covers and artist photos**: Navidrome's blue disc or a grey star stayed in lists while now playing had the real
  picture, and a cover replaced on Emby or Jellyfin kept showing the old one.
- **Two users of one server**: similar albums, artists and songs now come from the right user's library.
- **Android Auto**: every album and artist of a large library can be reached, and songs streamed in a lower quality
  show their progress bar.
- **ListenBrainz / Koito couldn't be removed**, and Last.fm now gets the album for compilations and songs with
  several artists.
- **Search lists the best answers first**: the exact name, then names that start with what you typed.
- **Play no longer changes colour while a page opens**, and the album page's background fades without stripes.
- **The mini player went empty** after swiping the only song.
- **TV:** Home kept saying "Syncing your library" after the sync had finished, and some buttons couldn't be reached
  with the remote.
