### Changed
- **Genre colours that fit the music.** Genre tiles and genre radios are now coloured by how the music feels: hard and fast genres (drum and bass, dubstep, metal, techno…) in reds, calm ones (ambient, jazz, liquid funk, deep house…) in blues and greens, the rest in warm oranges and purples.
- **All genres A to Z.** The page of all genres is now in alphabetical order; Search and Home still show the genres with the most songs first.
