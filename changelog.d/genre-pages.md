### Changed
- **Genre pages.** Tapping a genre now opens a page with all its songs. Play, Shuffle or a song there starts the genre's radio: 30 songs picked at random (the one you tapped first), with 30 more added each time the queue is about to end, until you play something else. The whole genre is never put in the queue.
- **Dynamic colours only on Play.** On album and playlist pages, only the Play button takes the cover's colour now, as on artist pages.
