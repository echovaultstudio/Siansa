### Added
- **Siansa Beats can answer what songs sound like.** With Siansa Beats 0.25, which takes over AudioMuse-AI's analysis, Siansa asks it first for similar songs, albums and artists, Song path, blended and mood radios, Your sound, and the tempo, key and moods DJ mixes choose by. With Siansa Beats 0.26 it also finds music from a description: the mood radios, Flow's "Describe what you want to hear", vibes and their sliders, and cards made from a sound. AudioMuse-AI then needn't be set up in Siansa for any of those. Without Siansa Beats everything else works as before.

### Changed
- **A DJ mix needs Siansa Beats, and only Siansa Beats.** Its songs are put in order by the tempo Siansa Beats finds from each song's beats, never by AudioMuse-AI's (which read drum and bass as 89 or 110 BPM). AudioMuse-AI alone, or with the old Siansa plugin, no longer offers DJ mixes; Steady radio, similar songs and everything else still work with it.
