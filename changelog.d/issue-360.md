### Changed
- **Musix is now Siansa** (say "SHEE-an-sa", Irish for melody and harmony), with a new icon: a glassy orb with a
  sine wave running through it, on black. Siansa installs as a new app next to Musix, so it starts empty. To
  bring your settings across, save a backup in Musix (Settings › Backup) and restore it in Siansa, then sign
  in again and uninstall Musix.
