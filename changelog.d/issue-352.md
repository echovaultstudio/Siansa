### Added
- **Music files on your phone.** Add a folder in **Settings › Servers and services › Files on this phone** and its songs show in your library, search and genre radios next to your server's, and play straight from the phone, also offline. Siansa reads only the folders you pick. Songs that are on your server too are matched to it (by their MusicBrainz IDs, or title, artist and length), so they join AudioMuse mixes and radios; songs only on the phone play normally but stay out of those. For files without MusicBrainz IDs, Siansa can look them up on MusicBrainz (you can turn this off). Show or hide them with the eye in Home's source filter.
