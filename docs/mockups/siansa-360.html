Siansa (SHEE-an-sa), Irish for melody and harmony. Three simple, strong marks in the app's colours: deep plum, soft violet and the amber you press to play. Each one is shown as Android shows it: round and squircle masks, small, and the one-colour themed icon (third), plus the wordmark on light.
