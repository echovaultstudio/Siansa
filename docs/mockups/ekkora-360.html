  • Colours stay the same. Plum #110D1C, violet #A891FF and amber #FFB547, so screens, buttons and themes carry over unchanged.
  • Amber means "play", as it does across the app today, which is why the play button (B) and the travelling light (C) are amber and everything else is violet.
  • Type is Inter Display, a clean modern sans with the same calm feel as the app's text.
  • A (Echo bars) keeps the current icon's sunrise bars, the smallest change for people who know Musix.
  • B and C are the boldest new marks: simple enough for a tiny notification icon and a big splash alike.
  • All six fit Android's adaptive-icon safe zone, so no launcher will crop them.
