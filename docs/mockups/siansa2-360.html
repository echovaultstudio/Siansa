Siansa: four marks with music built in

Each concept fuses at least two of: records, instruments, notes, frequency and AI. Each is drawn with real light and depth, in its own colours rather than the current theme. Every one is also shown small, round, tiny, and as the one-colour Android themed icon, so it holds up everywhere.

siansa
RecordFrequencyS

A vinyl record caught in the light: the glare across its grooves is a glowing S, shifting from cyan to magenta like the sheen on a real record.

The Irish harp, drawn in gold, whose strings are a frequency spectrum glowing from green to violet. The harp's shape is already a spectrum: short strings high, long strings low.

  • Groove: the most iconic and "music app" at a glance. A record anyone recognises, made ownable by the S of light.
  • Neural note: says AI music most directly. The constellation flag is distinctive and modern.
  • Spectrum harp: the most premium and the most Irish; it ties the name's meaning (harmony) to an instrument. Note: the Irish State harp is a protected emblem, so the final harp must stay clearly stylised, as here, not a copy of it.
  • Listening orb: the freshest and most "AI assistant", and the lightest on a home screen.
