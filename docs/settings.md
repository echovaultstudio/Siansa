## Connections

Everything Siansa connects to, each a card that folds open. **Test** tries what's typed; **Save** keeps every change.

- **Servers**: each music server login (its own library, history and settings), with **Switch to it** and **Remove**
  for the others; **Audiobookshelf**; and **Add a music server**. See [Servers, users and
  libraries](servers-and-users.md) and [Audiobooks and podcasts](books.md).
- **Services**: **AudioMuse-AI** ([how](audiomuse.md)) and the **AI** service, its key and model ([AI features](ai.md)).

## Playback

**Equaliser**; **Crossfade and levelling** (levelling, crossfades, beat-matched blends); **Streaming quality**; **Song
radio** (Stay close, A little, Wander); **Keep playing** (Off, Steady or DJ); **Wind down** (10, 20 or 30 calm songs);
and how far the book player skips. See [Playback](playback.md) and [Discovery](discovery.md).

## Data

**Load songs ahead**, the **Song cache**, **Downloads**, **Covers on the phone** (Download all covers, the image
cache), and what the library and logs take. See [Playback](playback.md#quality-cache-and-downloads).

## Notices and Cards

**Notices** (Pinned, Automatic or Off for each kind) and **Cards** (Made for you and Custom cards). See [Home and your
mixes](home-and-mixes.md#your-cards). The source filter and Arrange Home are in Home's round menu.

## Auto Generated Mixes

Today's mix, your vibes, and **Use the moment**. See [Home and your mixes](home-and-mixes.md#vibes-and-todays-mix).

## Style

- **Themes**: Auto, Light or Dark; **Pure black**; a colour scheme, **Your colour** or your wallpaper's; the accent
  colour; and **Pages and cards** (Grey, Warm grey, Blue grey or Match theme).
- **Now playing**: its buttons, **Dynamic colours** (the controls take the cover's colour), and the **Play button
  slides** and what each way does.
- **Mini player**, **bottom bar** and **Home Menu**: drag to reorder, tap the eye to hide.
- **Home**: how many shortcuts (2–8).
- **Phone's top bar**: See-through, Black or Hidden.
- **Animated covers**: an album's cover.gif or cover.webp moves on its page and in now playing.

## Scrobbling and history

When a song counts, and where it's sent: your server, Last.fm, ListenBrainz or Koito. See [Your
listening](your-listening.md#scrobbling).

## Backup and restore

A backup holds your settings, vibes, mixes, history and layout, **never passwords, tokens or API keys**. **Save
backup**, **Restore**, or **Automatic backup** once a day (the last 7 kept).
