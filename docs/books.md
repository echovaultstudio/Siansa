# Audiobooks and podcasts

Siansa plays the audiobooks and podcasts on your [Audiobookshelf](https://www.audiobookshelf.org/) server, next to your
music. They stay apart from music: never in mixes, radios or scrobbling.

## Setting it up

**Settings › Connections › Servers › Audiobookshelf**: its address (`192.168.1.10:13378`) and an **API token** or your
user name and password, then **Test**. **Scan for new books** asks the server to look for new ones (admin logins only).
One Audiobookshelf login serves every music user.

## Where they show

- **Home**: the **Your books** and **Your podcasts** carousels (Continue, Up next, New), in Arrange Home.
- **Your Library**: the **Books** and **Podcasts** chips, sorted by Recents, Title, Author, Series or Recently added.
- **Search**: Books and Podcasts rows, by title, author or narrator.
- **A book's page**: its details and description, how far you are, **download**, **mark finished**, **Play** (carries
  on where you were), and the chapters or episodes.

## The book player

It opens and closes like now playing.

- **↺ and ↻** skip back and forward (10 s and 30 s to start with; 10–90 s under **Settings › Playback**). **⏮ ⏭** go
  to the last and next chapter.
- **Speed** (0.5× to 3×, per book), the **moon** to stop at the end of the chapter, and **Bookmark**.
- The mini player shows ↺ and ↻ while a book plays. Music and a book never play at once.

Where you are is saved on the server every 15 seconds and when you pause, so Audiobookshelf's own apps carry on from
the same place.
