# Discovery

## Song radio

Long-press a song and pick **Start a mix**, or slide from the play button in now playing. The radio starts with that
song, then keeps adding songs that sound like it. Pick **Steady** (the wave) or **DJ** (the turntable) order.

**Settings › Playback › Song radio** sets how far it wanders: **Stay close**, **A little** (the default) or **Wander**,
where each batch follows the song playing.

### Shape the mix

While a DJ radio plays, the queue has **Shape the mix**:

| Shape | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Steady vibes** (default) | Stays where it is. |
| **Steady build** | The energy climbs to the top over about eight songs. |
| **Euphoric** | Up to a high in three songs, and stays there. |
| **Wind down** | The energy falls to the calmest over about eight songs. |

**Surprises** (None, A few, Some, Lots) lets songs stray further, always in a key that still mixes. Both also apply to
Keep playing in DJ mode.

## Keep playing

**Settings › Playback › Keep playing**, for when the queue is about to end: **Off**, **Steady** (more songs like the
last one), or **DJ** (the same, ordered for smooth changes by tempo and key; needs AudioMuse-AI). Songs Siansa added
are marked in the queue.

## Similar music

- **Similar songs** and **Similar albums**, from a song's menu; **Fans also like** on an artist's page. Albums and
  artists that *sound* alike come from the Siansa plugin.
- On an album, **Play** carries on where you stopped (hold it to start over), and **Shuffle** shuffles. With
  AudioMuse-AI, **slide from Shuffle onto Play** for a **DJ mix of the album**: its own songs, in the order that blends
  best, with DJ blends.

## Song path

From a song's menu: **Song path…**. Pick a second song or a mood, and Siansa builds a queue that moves smoothly from
one sound to the other. **Follow the lyrics** moves by what the songs are about (needs lyrics analysed in
AudioMuse-AI).

## Flow

The **Flow** tab: **describe what you want to hear** ("tired but hopeful", "rainy Sunday") and Siansa makes a mix for
it. Then shape it with sliders: **Energy**, **Tempo**, **Familiar** (from artists you rarely play to your favourites),
and **Sound dials** towards or away from a sound (piano, acoustic, electronic…). Your vibes are kept.

## Search

By name as you type, and **by sound** once you pause ("dreamy synth pop"), where AudioMuse-AI finds songs that sound
like your words. Tap a section's title for every match.

## Roll the dice

One song from your library you've never played, at random. In Home's round menu.

## Artists you might like

Artists you don't have yet, like the ones you play most, from your server (Last.fm via Navidrome) and Deezer. Only
the artist's name is sent to Deezer. Lines join each to the artists you play that it's like.

## Rediscover

Albums you used to play a lot, but not lately.
