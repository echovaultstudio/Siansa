# Your Stations: radio and podcasts

Internet radio and podcasts by their RSS feed, in **Your Library**: stations under **Stations**, podcasts under
**Podcasts**.

## Adding them

- **From your server**: Navidrome's radio stations (added in its web page, under **Radios**) show by themselves, marked
  **From your server**.
- **Your own**: tap **+** at the top of Your Library, then **Add station** or **Add podcast**, and paste a stream
  link, a `.m3u`, `.m3u8` or `.pls` playlist, or a podcast's RSS feed. Siansa checks it plays before adding it.

What you add stays on this phone, for the user in use. **⋮ › Edit** changes a station's name, link or picture, **⋮ ›
Remove** takes it away.

Each station finds its own logo, from [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info) or the station's website.

## Listening

- A station plays **Live**, with what's on when the station sends it. It isn't scrobbled, liked or crossfaded.
- A podcast's page lists its episodes, newest first. Episodes are never crossfaded, scrobbled or cached.
- Both need a connection and can't be downloaded. HLS streams work; very old Shoutcast servers may not.
