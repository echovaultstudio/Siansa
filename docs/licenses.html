Open source

Siansa is free software, under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. We believe an app that holds your server login should be open to read, and that music players for self-hosted servers are better built together. The code is on GitHub, and so is the AudioMuse-AI plugin (AGPL 3). Bug reports and pull requests are welcome: the issues take reports and ideas, and the docs explain every part of the app.

It's built on open-source software too. These are the libraries inside the Android app, and their licences. Thank you to everyone who makes them.

In the app

FFmpeg decoder for Media3

Jellyfin · org.jellyfin.media3:media3-ffmpeg-decoder

Plays the formats a phone can't play itself, such as ALAC. It contains FFmpeg (LGPL 2.1 or later, with parts under the GPL).
