# Home and your mixes

## Home

Home is built from sections you can reorder, hide and restyle with **Arrange Home**, in the round menu at the top.

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Shortcuts** | What you played from last: albums, playlists, mixes, Liked songs (2–8, under **Settings › Style › Home**). |
| **Today's mix** | The day's mix, for the vibe of the hour (below). |
| **Your mixes carousel** | Three or four mixes of your own for each part of the day. |
| **Made for you** | Your sound, On repeat, Discover for you, and cards of your own. |
| **Rediscover** | Albums you used to play a lot and haven't in a while. |
| **Radios** | Mood radios: Chill, Energy, Focus, Feel good, Party, Late night and more. |
| **Your playlists** | Your server's playlists, and ones kept on the phone. |
| **Recently played**, **Recently added**, **Random picks** | Albums. |
| **Artist radios**, **Your genres** | Radios from artists you play and from your genres. |

**Arrange Home** also sets the **Spacing** between sections, and each section's **cog** gives it another look: **Tiles**,
**Banner**, **List**, **Play button** or **Cover rows**, with or without its title. **Reset** puts it all back.

**Home's menu** (the round button): **Roll the dice**, **Play on…** (TVs and speakers), **Sources** (the source filter)
and **Arrange Home**. Tap it, or press and slide down to one and let go. Reorder it under **Settings › Style › Home
Menu**.

## Notices

Chips at the top of Home, only when there's something to say: a failed library sync, a book to carry on, new albums,
last month's report, new artists for you, Liked songs. They go away by themselves. Under **Settings › Notices and
Cards › Notices**, set each kind to **Pinned**, **Automatic** or **Off**.

## Vibes and today's mix

A **vibe** covers part of the day: Morning, Daytime, Evening and Night to start with. The vibe of the hour makes
**today's mix**. Change them under **Settings › Auto Generated Mixes**:

- **Name**, **Days** and **hours**. When several fit, the most specific wins.
- **Guidance for the AI**: how this time should feel. The AI writes the mix's title and sound search from it
  ([AI features](ai.md)).
- **Fallback sound search**, for when AI is off, and **Never use these words**.
- **Mix length**, **Don't repeat songs played in the last … days**, and **Surprise**: how much comes from outside your
  usual artists.

At the top of that page, today's mix has its own settings for whichever vibe makes it. **Auto update** (on by
default) makes a new mix as the day and the vibe move on; off, the mix stays until you press refresh. **Songs**,
**Surprise**, **Leave out most played** and **Days before a repeat** can each follow the vibe or have their own value.

The **Use the moment** panel lets vibes take the weather, the light, whether you're moving, and your headphones or car
into account ([AI features](ai.md#situation)).

How the songs are picked: [How it works](how-it-works.md#todays-mix-and-vibes).

**Without AudioMuse-AI**, the mixes made by sound are hidden (today's mix, the carousel, Your sound and Discover for
you). On repeat, made from your plays, stays.

## Your mixes carousel

**Home carousel** (from Arrange Home): three or four mixes for each part of the day. Give each a name and a sound
("upbeat energetic groove, punchy drums"). They never share songs. Each carousel is for all week, or for the days you
pick.

## Made for you

- **Your sound**: songs closest to the sound you play most (AudioMuse-AI's sonic fingerprint).
- **On repeat**: what you played most in the last 30 days.
- **Discover for you**: songs that sound like your taste, from artists you don't play much.
- **Cards of your own** (**Settings › Notices and Cards › Cards › Made for you**): a genre or a period, with new songs
  every day.

Every mix, radio and card has **Save as playlist**, on your server or on this phone only. The queue can be saved the
same way, from now playing.

## Your cards

Custom cards (**Settings › Notices and Cards › Cards › Custom cards**), shown in Home's **Your cards** section.

- **Shows**: **Songs** or **Albums**; with Audiobookshelf, also **Audiobooks**, **Podcast episodes** or **Keep
  listening**.
- **Comes from**: your library, by rules, or **By sound**: describe it ("rainy jazz piano") and AudioMuse-AI finds it.
- **Rules**: title, album, artist, genre, record label, release type, year, length, play count, times skipped, added,
  played, favourite, compilation. Keep what matches **every** rule or **any**. Text takes several values: "Genre
  contains House, Techno". For example, an album card with **Record label contains Ninja Tune** and **Year is 2024**.
  **Played not in the last 90 days** makes a "not heard in a while" card.
- **Order** (random gives a new set each day), **How many** (up to 500), and its **Look on Home**: tiles, album row,
  banner, list, play button or **Cover rows** (covers with three lines beside them, three to a column).

Tap a card's title for everything on it.

## Radios

- **Mood radios**: AudioMuse-AI sound searches, kept varied.
- **Genre radios**: songs of that genre from your library.
- **Artist radios**: a few of the artist's songs, then songs that sound like them (AudioMuse-AI), with one of theirs
  every fourth song. Without AudioMuse-AI, your server's similar songs.

## Your Library

Chips narrow it down: **Playlists**, **Music** (with **Albums**, **Artists**, **Songs**), **Mixes**, **Favourites**,
**Your listening** and **Stations**. They come in the order you last used them, and each keeps its own sort and its
own list or grid. **+** adds a station or a podcast feed ([Your Stations](stations.md)).

Long lists have a handle on the right: drag it to move through the whole list, with a letter or a month beside your
finger.
