# Home and your mixes

Home is built from sections you can reorder and hide (**Arrange Home**, in the menu at the top of Home).

## The top of Home

The top of Home is the row of notices (see [Notices](#notices)) with a round menu button at its end, in line with
the chips and the same height. When there are more chips than fit, they scroll and fade out just before the button.
With no notices, or notices turned off, the button sits on its own.

## Home's menu

The round button opens the menu in the top row itself: the notice chips shrink and fade away and four pills of their
own shape take their place, exactly the chips' height and a small gap apart, one option each (in the order set under
Settings › Home page › Home menu). Nothing on the page is covered, and nothing moves. The names are read out by TalkBack:

- **Roll the dice** (the dice) plays a song you've never played (see [Roll the dice](discovery.md)). While it finds
  one, the dice tumbles in the menu button.
- **Play on…** (the cast mark) opens the list of TVs and speakers to play on: Chromecast, Google TV, Cast speakers and
  DLNA ones. While something plays on one, its pill is filled with the accent colour (amber unless you picked another
  in Themes) and the menu button has a small dot in the same colour; to stop, open Play on… and pick **This phone**.
- **Sources** (the circle of arrows, as on Sync all) opens the Sources sheet. Its pill is filled with the accent colour
  while the filter hides something.
- **Arrange Home** (the three lines) opens Arrange, for the order and looks of Home's sections.

Tap the button and then one of them, or do it in one go: press the button, slide your finger along the pills to one (it
fills and grows a touch, with a light tick as you pass each) and let go. Letting go anywhere else closes the menu, and so does
a tap on the button (its lines become a cross while the menu is open) or anywhere on the page. The button's three lines
are always in the accent colour; the button has a thin ring in the same colour while the source filter hides something.

Their order is yours: **Settings › Home page › Home menu**, hold a row and drag it.

## Arrange Home

Arrange is a sheet of its own (the Sources sheet is another), in three parts: **Cards**, **Spacing** and **Sections**.

**Cards** is how every coloured card on Home is painted — Today's mix, the book and podcast cards, Made for you,
Radios and the other generated tiles; never the shortcut grid, whose tiles are plain already. Four small cards show
the choice:

| Look | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Vivid** | Each card in its own full colour (the default). |
| **Soft** | The same colours at a third of their strength over the dark surface: the card keeps its colour, the page keeps its calm. |
| **Plain** | Every card the same grey as the shortcut tiles; colour only in the artwork and the play button. |
| **From the artwork** | A muted colour taken from the card's own artwork, as the listening pages' headers do; a card without artwork is painted soft. |

**Spacing** sets the gap between every section at once, from Tight to Airy. **Sections** is a row per section, with
the handle at the left to move it, the eye to hide it and the cog for its look. **Reset** puts the order, the hidden
sections, the spacing, the looks and the cards' colour back.

The **cog** on a section changes its look, to any of the custom cards' looks:

| Look | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **As it is** | The section's own look (the default). |
| **Tiles** | A row of covers to swipe through. |
| **Banner** | One wide card with the first covers and a button. |
| **List** | The first five, listed; the title opens the rest. |
| **Play button** | A slim card: its ▶ plays the section; a tap anywhere else on the card opens it. |
| **Cover rows** | Covers with their details beside them, three to a column, to swipe through: the same look as a custom card's. The cover plays that album or opens that item; the row opens it. Books and podcasts show how far you are along the cover. A radio or genre shows its colours instead of a cover. |
| **Shortcut grid** | Small tiles two to a row, like Shortcuts. Pick **2, 4, 6 or 8 tiles** under it in the same dialog. A tile opens its item; the title opens the rest. Shortcuts itself has no such choice: it's the grid already, and its count is under Settings › Home page › Top of Home. |

A section in another look says which under its name. **Play** in a banner or the ▶ of a play button card plays the section:
today's mix or the carousel's first mix where they left off, or the albums of Rediscover, Recently played, Recently
added and Random picks in order. Sections of radios, playlists, genres, books and Made for you open instead. **Your
cards** keep their own looks: their cog opens **Settings › Home page › Cards**.

The same cog has **Show its title**: turn it off and the section shows without the heading above it ("Your evening",
"Recently played"), keeping the gap where it was. And its **Colour**: a card shows the section's cards in the chosen
look, and **As Home** follows the choice made under Cards, or pick one for this section alone (Shortcuts has none).

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Shortcuts** | The albums, playlists, mixes and Liked songs you played from last, newest first: 8 of them, or as many as you pick from 2 to 8 under **Settings › Home page › Top of Home**. Until there's enough of that, today's mixes, your playlists and recent albums. |
| **Today's mix** | The day's mix, made for the vibe of the hour it's made in (see below). |
| **Your mixes carousel** | Three or four mixes of your own for each part of the day, side by side. |
| **Made for you** | Your sound, On repeat, Discover for you, then cards of your own. |
| **Rediscover** | Albums you used to play a lot and haven't in a while. |
| **Radios** | Mood radios: Chill, Energy, Focus, Feel good, Party, Melancholy, Workout, Late night, Acoustic, Romance. |
| **Your playlists** | Playlists on your server and ones kept only on the phone. |
| **Recently played** | Albums you played lately, only from music the source filter shows. |
| **Artist radios** | Artists you played lately, mixed with artists like them. |
| **Recently added** | The newest albums on your server. |
| **Your genres** | Genre radios from your library: the songs tagged with that genre. With Siansa Beats, every song that sounds like it, whatever its tags say (a remix tagged with its album's genre, but sounding like trance, plays in Trance), as long as the genre is among the song's three best guesses (Siansa Beats 0.36: a faint fourth guess doesn't count); without Siansa Beats, or until it has heard enough of them, the tags as always. |
| **Random picks** | A random selection to dig into. |

## Notices

A row of chips at the top of Home, before the menu button, only when there's something to say. Tap a chip to open it. Chips come and go on
their own: a finished book, albums older than three days or last month's report once the week is over drop out.

| Notice | When it shows | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Library sync failed** | The last library sync didn't finish. | Connections |
| **Carry on: …** | A book or podcast episode you played in the last week and haven't finished. | The book |
| **3 new albums** | Albums added to your library in the last 30 days. It shows as soon as Home opens. | Recently added |
| **Your October** | The first week of a month: last month's listening. | The month's report |
| **4 artists for you** | New artists to try were found today. | Artists for you |
| **Liked songs** | Always, once you add it: it's **Off** until you set it to Automatic or Pinned. | Liked songs |

At most five show. By default they're ordered for you: what needs you first, then what you're in the middle of, then
what's new; among the new ones, the kinds you tap most come first. In **Settings › Home page › Top of Home** you can hide the
row, or set each kind to **Pinned**, **Automatic** or **Off**. Pinned kinds always show, first and in the order you
pin them, and are never pushed out by other notices; the automatic ones fill the places left. The notices are
always at the top of Home, so they're not in Arrange Home.

## Vibes and today's mix

A **vibe** covers part of the day. Siansa starts with Morning, Daytime, Evening and Night. When you open the app,
the vibe for the current hour builds **today's mix**. **Settings › Radios & mixes › Today's mix and vibes** lets you change them:

- **Name**, **Days** (or every day), and **hours** (0–23; a vibe from 22 to 5 runs past midnight). When several
  vibes fit, the most specific wins: one set for a day before an everyday one, one for some hours before an
  all-day one.
- **Guidance for the AI**: how this time of day should feel. With AI on, the AI writes a title and a sound search
  from it (see [AI features](ai.md)).
- **Fallback sound search**: used when AI is off or unavailable, for example "calm atmospheric night music, soft
  and slow".
- **Never use these words**: an AI answer with one of them is thrown away.
- **Mix length**, **Don't repeat songs played in the last … days**, and **Surprise**: how much of the mix comes
  from outside your usual artists.
- **Make a mix now** builds it straight away.

How the songs are picked is explained in [How it works](how-it-works.md#todays-mix-and-vibes).

**Today's mix** has settings of its own at the top of **Settings › Radios & mixes › Today's mix and vibes**, which apply to it whichever
vibe makes it:

- **Auto update** (on by default): the mix follows the time of day. A new one is made as the day or the hour's vibe
  moves on, when Home or the background next needs it, and when a tuning below changes. Off: the mix you last made
  with refresh stays on Home, whatever the day or the hour, until you press refresh again. Nothing is made for you;
  before the first, Home offers **Make it**.
- **Songs**, **Surprise**, **Leave out most played** and **Days before a repeat**: each either **Vibe's own** (what
  the vibe that makes the mix says) or a value of its own, so one place tunes the mix you see first.

**When mixes are made.** With Auto update on, today's mix is made when Home or the background first needs it, and
again when the hour's vibe moves on; refresh makes a new one at any time. The
carousel's mixes are made for each part of the day, the first time Home shows them. Showing or hiding music with
the source filter doesn't make mixes again; tap refresh to make one for the music shown now.

**Without AudioMuse-AI** set up for the user in use, the AI mixes are hidden: today's mix, the carousel, the vibe
tiles, Your sound and Discover for you. On repeat, made from your plays, stays.

The **Use the moment** panel on the same page lets vibes take the weather, whether you're moving, the light and your
headphones or car into account. See [AI features](ai.md#situation).

## Saving a mix as a playlist

Every mix, radio and Made for you card has a **Save as playlist** button next to **Add all to queue** (a filled
circle with a plus; a song's own "Add to playlist" is an outlined one). Name it (the mix's name to start with) and
choose **On your server**, so it syncs with your other apps, or **On this phone only**. You can save the play queue
the same way, from the button next to **Up next** in now playing.

## Your mixes carousel

**Home carousel** (from Arrange Home): three or four mixes for each part of the day. Give each a name and what it
should sound like, for example "upbeat energetic groove, punchy drums". They never share songs, so make them
different. The fourth is optional. Changes make today's mixes again the next time you open Home.

Each carousel is for **All week**, or only for the days you pick. Two carousels can't have the same hours on the
same day; Siansa tells you which one already has them.

## Made for you

- **Your sound**: songs closest to the sound of the music you play most (AudioMuse-AI's sonic fingerprint; only
  with AudioMuse-AI).
- **On repeat**: the songs you played most in the last 30 days, from your server's play counts, this phone and
  Koito, ListenBrainz or Last.fm when you use them. It opens straight away and checks your server for changes while
  the cog turns, like a playlist. Until there's enough to go on, it isn't shown.
- **Discover for you**: songs that sound like your taste, from artists you don't play much (only with AudioMuse-AI).
- **Cards of your own** (**Settings › Home page › Cards › Made for you**): pick what a card shows (for example a genre or a
  period) and name it. Each card gets new songs every day.

## Your cards

Custom cards you make yourself (**Settings › Home page › Cards › Custom cards**), shown on Home in its **Your cards** section
(move it under Arrange Home; the cards' own order is set in Settings). **Add a custom card** or tap one to change it,
on a page of its own:

- **Shows**: **Songs**, or whole **Albums**. Playing an album card plays its first albums in turn; its page lists
  every album. With Audiobookshelf set up, also **Audiobooks**, **Podcast episodes** (the newest few of your
  podcasts) or **Keep listening** (books and episodes you're partway through, the most recent first). These have no
  rules, only an order (recently added, A to Z or random) and how many, and show as Cover rows.
- **Comes from** (songs only): **From your library**, by rules, **By sound**: describe a sound ("rainy jazz
  piano") and AudioMuse-AI finds songs that match (only with AudioMuse-AI), or **A favourite radio**: one of the
  radios you hearted (below), its songs new each day.
- **Rules**: each says what to keep or leave out, and the card keeps what matches **every rule** or **any rule**.
  Add one with **Add a rule**, then pick how it compares and the value:

  | Rule | Compares |
  |---|---|
  | Song title, Album, Artist, Genre, Record label, Release type | contains, doesn't contain, is, isn't |
  | Year, Length (minutes), Songs on it, Play count, Times skipped | is, isn't, at least, at most, between |
  | Added, Played (days) | in the last, not in the last |
  | Favourite, Compilation | yes, no |

  Text takes several values separated by commas: "Genre contains House, Techno" keeps either, "Release type doesn't
  contain Live, Remix" leaves both out. The genres, record labels and release types in your library are there to
  tap. A song's record label, release type and date added are its album's. Song title and Times skipped are for
  song cards only; Songs on it for album cards. For example, an album card with **Record label contains Ninja Tune**
  and **Year is 2024** shows every 2024 album on that label.

  **Play count** and **Played** (when it was last played) for a song come from what Siansa knows: this phone's own
  history (about the last two months) and your server's count for songs played lately. A song played only long ago
  on another device may show as never played. **Played not in the last 90 days** also keeps songs never played, so
  it makes a "not heard in a while" card. **Times skipped** counts the skips on this phone. An album's play count is
  the server's, or its songs' added up when that's higher.
- **Order**: random (a new set every day, with a few songs per artist at most), newest or oldest first, recently
  added, most played (a song's own plays first), or A to Z; and **How many, at most** (up to 500).
- **Look on Home**: **Song tiles** (a row of covers; for an album card, **Album tiles**), **Album row** (the albums
  the songs come from), **Banner** (one wide card with the first covers and Play and Shuffle), **Song list** (the
  first five; for an album card, **Album list**), **Play button** (a slim card: its ▶ plays it, the rest opens it),
  **Cover rows** (below) and **Shortcut grid** (small tiles two to a row, like Shortcuts: pick 2, 4, 6 or 8; a song's
  tile plays it, an album's opens it).

**Cover rows**: each row is a cover with three lines beside it, three rows to a column; swipe sideways for the next
three (the dots under the card show which of them you're on), and tap the card's title for all of them.

| Card holds | First line | Second line | Third line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Albums | the artist | the album | how many songs, the year |
| Songs | the artist | the song | its album, its length |
| Audiobooks | the author | the book | its length, the time left |
| Podcast episodes | the podcast | the episode (with its number) | its length, the time left, or "new" |

Tap a cover to play it or carry on where you stopped; tap it again to pause. Tap the rest of the row to open it.
**⋮**, or press and hold the row, for more: Add to queue and Go to artist for an album; Play next, Add to queue and
Go to album for a song; Play from the start for a book. Books and episodes you've started show how far you've got
along the bottom of the cover. The one playing has a pause sign over its cover and a ♪ before its first line.

A card made before rules keeps working: its genre, years, artist, album, words and favourites become rules the
first time you change it. A random card's page has a button for a new set right away. Tap a card's title (or a play button card
anywhere but its ▶) for everything on it, ready to play or save as a playlist. A card can be kept but taken off
Home with its switch in Settings.

## Radios

- **Mood radios** are AudioMuse-AI sound searches, kept varied. Their page has the radio's name on its cover, and its
  Play takes the cover's colour with Dynamic colours on. Every page whose cover Siansa draws is the same: a radio, a
  mood playlist, a vibe, a Made for you card, your own cards and a song path.
- **New picks**, on the right of the line under the cover, picks the songs again, on every page that can: radios,
  "More like" a playlist, mood playlists and cards.
- **Favourite radios**: the heart on a radio's page (a mood radio, a genre, "More like" a playlist) keeps it in Your
  Library under **Favourites**. Right after you heart one, **Make a Home card** makes a custom card of it, or pick it
  later under **Comes from › A favourite radio** in a card.
- **On every page of songs**, once the cover and buttons have scrolled away, the title bar has its **Play** (a radio's
  **radio**, and its heart). They grow into the bar the same way on every page: albums, playlists, radios, genres,
  artists, books and podcasts.
- **Genres** open a page with every song of the genre on your phone, to look
  through. With Siansa Beats (0.19 or later), it says what the genre is, with a little of its history: the opening of
  its Wikipedia article, linked under it. Under that, one line: how many songs it has on the left, and on the right
  its heart beside its one button, the **radio** (round, in the genre's colour). The radio or a song there starts the genre's radio: 30 of its songs at random (the song you
  tapped first), the songs Siansa Beats is sure sound like the genre far more often than the faint ones (with Siansa Beats 0.36; by the tags every song is as likely), and 30 more of the genre each time the queue comes to its end, for as long as you don't play
  something else or add your own songs after it. The whole genre never goes into the queue, as some have thousands
  of songs. A genre's tile and page are coloured by its feel: red for hard and fast, blue or green for calm.
- **Artist radios** start with a few of the artist's songs. With AudioMuse-AI set up, the rest are songs that
  sound like the artist (AudioMuse blends a dozen of their songs), with one of their own every fourth song. Without
  it, they're your music server's similar songs (Navidrome's come from Last.fm or other agents it has); if the
  server has none, the artist's own songs. The same from Home and from an artist's page. In the queue, the songs
  AudioMuse-AI picked carry its mark, as a song radio's do.

## Your Library

The chips at the top narrow the list down: **Playlists**, **Music**, **Mixes**, **Favourites**, **Your listening**
and **Stations**. **Favourites** has Liked songs, your favourite albums and artists, and each radio you hearted.

- **Music** holds your albums, artists and songs; tap **Albums**, **Artists** or **Songs** under it to see one kind.
  **Songs** lists every song in the libraries you sync; tap one to play it and the songs after it.
- **Stations** lists your radio stations ([Your Stations](stations.md)): your server's and the ones you added, each
  with its logo. Tap one to play it live; **⋮** has **Edit** and **Remove**. Podcasts you add by their RSS link are
  under **Podcasts**, which shows whenever you have a podcast. Add either with **+ › Add station** or
  **+ › Add podcast** at the top of Your Library.
- **The chips come in the order you last opened them**, so the ones you use most are at the front. When more chips
  are hidden to the right, the row fades there with a **›** button that scrolls on.
- **Each chip keeps its own sort** (Recents, Alphabetical, Most played…) until you change it, also after Siansa
  restarts. Sorting Music by name doesn't change how Playlists are sorted.
- **Liked songs and playlists sort the same way**: the arrows under the title, with the order's name, open **Sort by**
  (Recently liked or Playlist order, Last played, Date added, Title, Artist). Each keeps its order.
- **Each view keeps its own list or grid** (the button next to the sort) until you change it, also after Siansa
  restarts: Playlists can be a grid while Music's Artists stay a list. Albums, Artists and Songs under Music each
  keep their own.

## Long lists

Liked songs, playlists, albums, favourite albums and artists, and the full lists behind Home's shelves have a
handle on the right, like Your Library. Drag it to move through the whole list at once; a label next to your
finger says where you are: a letter when the list is A to Z, a month when it's by date added or last played, or
the place in the list.
