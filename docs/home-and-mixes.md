# Home and your mixes

Home is built from sections you can reorder and hide (**Arrange Home**, from the filter button at the top of Home).
Its **Spacing** slider sets the gap between every section at once, from Tight to Airy; **Reset** puts the order,
the hidden sections, the spacing and the looks back.

The **cog** on a section changes its look, to any of the custom cards' looks:

| Look | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **As it is** | The section's own look (the default). |
| **Tiles** | A row of covers to swipe through. |
| **Banner** | One wide card with the first covers and a button. |
| **List** | The first five, listed; the title opens the rest. |
| **Play button** | A slim card: its ▶ plays the section; a tap anywhere else on the card opens it. |

A section in another look says which under its name. **Play** in a banner or the ▶ of a play button card plays the section:
today's mix or the carousel's first mix where they left off, or the albums of Rediscover, Recently played, Recently
added and Random picks in order. Sections of radios, playlists, genres, books and Made for you open instead. **Your
cards** keep their own looks: their cog opens **Settings › Home › Cards**.

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Shortcuts** | Liked songs first, then the albums, playlists and mixes you played from last. Until there's enough of that, today's mixes, your playlists and recent albums. |
| **Today's mix** | The day's mix, made for the vibe of the hour it's made in (see below). |
| **Your mixes carousel** | Three or four mixes of your own for each part of the day, side by side. |
| **Made for you** | Your sound, On repeat, Discover for you, then cards of your own. |
| **Rediscover** | Albums you used to play a lot and haven't in a while. |
| **Radios** | Mood radios: Chill, Energy, Focus, Feel good, Party, Melancholy, Workout, Late night, Acoustic, Romance. |
| **Your playlists** | Playlists on your server and ones kept only on the phone. |
| **Recently played** | Albums you played lately, only from music the source filter shows. |
| **Artist radios** | Artists you played lately, mixed with artists like them. |
| **Recently added** | The newest albums on your server. |
| **Your genres** | Genre radios from your library. |
| **Random picks** | A random selection to dig into. |

## Vibes and today's mix

A **vibe** covers part of the day. Siansa starts with Morning, Daytime, Evening and Night. When you open the app,
the vibe for the current hour builds **today's mix**. **Settings › Mixes and vibes** lets you change them:

- **Name**, **Days** (or every day), and **hours** (0–23; a vibe from 22 to 5 runs past midnight). When several
  vibes fit, the most specific wins: one set for a day before an everyday one, one for some hours before an
  all-day one.
- **Guidance for the AI**: how this time of day should feel. With AI on, the AI writes a title and a sound search
  from it (see [AI features](ai.md)).
- **Fallback sound search**: used when AI is off or unavailable, for example "calm atmospheric night music, soft
  and slow".
- **Never use these words**: an AI answer with one of them is thrown away.
- **Mix length**, **Don't repeat songs played in the last … days**, and **Surprise**: how much of the mix comes
  from outside your usual artists.
- **Make a mix now** builds it straight away.

How the songs are picked is explained in [How it works](how-it-works.md#todays-mix-and-vibes).

**Today's mix** has settings of its own at the top of **Settings › Mixes and vibes**, which apply to it whichever
vibe makes it:

- **Auto update** (on by default): the mix follows the time of day. A new one is made as the day or the hour's vibe
  moves on, when Home or the background next needs it, and when a tuning below changes. Off: the mix you last made
  with refresh stays on Home, whatever the day or the hour, until you press refresh again. Nothing is made for you;
  before the first, Home offers **Make it**.
- **Songs**, **Surprise**, **Leave out most played** and **Days before a repeat**: each either **Vibe's own** (what
  the vibe that makes the mix says) or a value of its own, so one place tunes the mix you see first.

**When mixes are made.** With Auto update on, today's mix is made when Home or the background first needs it, and
again when the hour's vibe moves on; refresh makes a new one at any time. The
carousel's mixes are made for each part of the day, the first time Home shows them. Showing or hiding music with
the source filter doesn't make mixes again; tap refresh to make one for the music shown now.

**Without AudioMuse-AI** set up for the user in use, the AI mixes are hidden: today's mix, the carousel, the vibe
tiles, Your sound and Discover for you. On repeat, made from your plays, stays.

The **Use the moment** panel on the same page lets vibes take the weather, whether you're moving, the light and your
headphones or car into account. See [AI features](ai.md#situation).

## Saving a mix as a playlist

Every mix, radio and Made for you card has a **Save as playlist** button next to **Add all to queue** (a filled
circle with a plus; a song's own "Add to playlist" is an outlined one). Name it (the mix's name to start with) and
choose **On your server**, so it syncs with your other apps, or **On this phone only**. You can save the play queue
the same way, from the button next to **Up next** in now playing.

## Your mixes carousel

**Home carousel** (from Arrange Home): three or four mixes for each part of the day. Give each a name and what it
should sound like, for example "upbeat energetic groove, punchy drums". They never share songs, so make them
different. The fourth is optional. Changes make today's mixes again the next time you open Home.

Each carousel is for **All week**, or only for the days you pick. Two carousels can't have the same hours on the
same day; Siansa tells you which one already has them.

## Made for you

- **Your sound**: songs closest to the sound of the music you play most (AudioMuse-AI's sonic fingerprint; only
  with AudioMuse-AI).
- **On repeat**: the songs you played most in the last 30 days, from your server's play counts, this phone and
  Koito, ListenBrainz or Last.fm when you use them. It opens straight away and checks your server for changes while
  the cog turns, like a playlist. Until there's enough to go on, it isn't shown.
- **Discover for you**: songs that sound like your taste, from artists you don't play much (only with AudioMuse-AI).
- **Cards of your own** (**Settings › Home › Cards › Made for you**): pick what a card shows (for example a genre or a
  period) and name it. Each card gets new songs every day.

## Your cards

Custom cards you make yourself (**Settings › Home › Cards › Custom cards**), shown on Home in its **Your cards** section
(move it under Arrange Home; the cards' own order is set in Settings). **Add a custom card** or tap one to change it,
on a page of its own:

- **Shows**: **Songs**, or whole **Albums**. Playing an album card plays its first albums in turn; its page lists
  every album.
- **Comes from** (songs only): **From your library**, by rules, or **By sound**: describe a sound ("rainy jazz
  piano") and AudioMuse-AI finds songs that match (only with AudioMuse-AI).
- **Rules**: each says what to keep or leave out, and the card keeps what matches **every rule** or **any rule**.
  Add one with **Add a rule**, then pick how it compares and the value:

  | Rule | Compares |
  |---|---|
  | Song title, Album, Artist, Genre, Record label, Release type | contains, doesn't contain, is, isn't |
  | Year, Length (minutes), Songs on it, Play count, Times skipped | is, isn't, at least, at most, between |
  | Added, Played (days) | in the last, not in the last |
  | Favourite, Compilation | yes, no |

  Text takes several values separated by commas: "Genre contains House, Techno" keeps either, "Release type doesn't
  contain Live, Remix" leaves both out. The genres, record labels and release types in your library are there to
  tap. A song's record label, release type and date added are its album's. Song title and Times skipped are for
  song cards only; Songs on it for album cards. For example, an album card with **Record label contains Ninja Tune**
  and **Year is 2024** shows every 2024 album on that label.

  **Play count** and **Played** (when it was last played) for a song come from what Siansa knows: this phone's own
  history (about the last two months) and your server's count for songs played lately. A song played only long ago
  on another device may show as never played. **Played not in the last 90 days** also keeps songs never played, so
  it makes a "not heard in a while" card. **Times skipped** counts the skips on this phone. An album's play count is
  the server's, or its songs' added up when that's higher.
- **Order**: random (a new set every day, with a few songs per artist at most), newest or oldest first, recently
  added, most played (a song's own plays first), or A to Z; and **How many, at most** (up to 500).
- **Look on Home**: **Song tiles** (a row of covers; for an album card, **Album tiles**), **Album row** (the albums
  the songs come from), **Banner** (one wide card with the first covers and Play and Shuffle), **Song list** (the
  first five; for an album card, **Album list**) and **Play button** (a slim card: its ▶ plays it, the rest opens it).

A card made before rules keeps working: its genre, years, artist, album, words and favourites become rules the
first time you change it. A random card's page has a button for a new set right away. Tap a card's title (or a play button card
anywhere but its ▶) for everything on it, ready to play or save as a playlist. A card can be kept but taken off
Home with its switch in Settings.

## Radios

- **Mood radios** are AudioMuse-AI sound searches, kept varied.
- **Genre radios** take songs of that genre from your synced library.
- **Artist radios** start from an artist and add artists like them.

## Your Library

The chips at the top narrow the list down: **Playlists**, **Music**, **Mixes**, **Favourites** and **Your listening**.

- **Music** holds your albums, artists and songs; tap **Albums**, **Artists** or **Songs** under it to see one kind.
  **Songs** lists every song in the libraries you sync; tap one to play it and the songs after it.
- **The chips come in the order you last opened them**, so the ones you use most are at the front. When more chips
  are hidden to the right, the row fades there with a **›** button that scrolls on.
- **Each chip keeps its own sort** (Recents, Alphabetical, Most played…) until you change it, also after Siansa
  restarts. Sorting Music by name doesn't change how Playlists are sorted.

## Long lists

Liked songs, playlists, albums, favourite albums and artists, and the full lists behind Home's shelves have a
handle on the right, like Your Library. Drag it to move through the whole list at once; a label next to your
finger says where you are: a letter when the list is A to Z, a month when it's by date added or last played, or
the place in the list.
